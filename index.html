How To Use This Tool

Global Controls (top bar)

The five controls in the top-right corner apply across every tab — they're the shared inputs that drive all the calculations.

Start / Months — sets the date range shown on the Swimlane tab only.

Training days / Ramp days — how long a newly hired rep takes to become fully productive. Training is the onboarding period; Ramp is the period after training where productivity scales up gradually (see the ramp curve in Monthly ARR Build). These two numbers add together — e.g. 30 + 90 = 120 days to full productivity.

FY end — the fiscal year-end month used for the Quota and Monthly ARR Build calculations. Defaults to December 2027.

Apply buying-season multiplier — toggles whether the Texas district buying calendar (see below) adjusts the monthly ARR figures. On by default.

Swimlane

The master timeline. Top section shows Product Development — grouped by module (Foundation, IEP, Integrations, LPAC) with each phase as a colored bar. Bottom section shows Sales Reps — each rep's Training → Ramping → Full Productivity progression based on their hire date and the global training/ramp settings.

The District Buying Season row near the top shows which months are strong for selling (BUY), slow (SLOW), consumed by STAAR testing (STAAR), or budget-cycle months (BUDGET) — see the dedicated section below.

Dashed teal lines mark GA milestones, derived live from the corresponding phase end-dates in Edit Phases. Hover any bar or band for details.

Quota & ARR Impact

Shows each rep's Avg ACV, Deals/mo, and Steady-State Quota (their full annual quota once fully ramped), alongside Base FY27 (the original mid-year model figure) and Current FY27 (live-calculated from the rep's current hire date, the ramp curve, and — if enabled — the buying-season multiplier).

You can edit a rep's hire date directly in this table — the totals at the top, the delta vs. base model, and every other tab update immediately. This is the fastest way to test "what if this hire slips a month?"

Monthly ARR Build

The most detailed view. The chart shows monthly (or cumulative) ARR contribution stacked by rep, against the $7.1M annual / $591K monthly target line. Click any bar to highlight it.

Below the chart, three tables: the ramp curve (productivity % by month-since-hire, which stretches or compresses based on your Training/Ramp day settings), ramp % per rep per month, and monthly quota ($) per rep with a team total row colored against the target.

Use Edit season multipliers to open an inline editor for the 12 monthly buying-season multipliers — change any value and everything recalculates.

Readiness Alignment

Answers: "Will we have enough ramped reps when a product hits GA?" Each GA milestone is a vertical line, colored green (2+ reps ramped), amber (1 rep), or red (0 reps) based on coverage at that date. Rep rows below show their hire → train → ramp → full progression on the same timeline.

The background shading and bottom strip show the buying-season overlay — useful for spotting cases where a rep's ramp period lands during STAAR testing season, when pipeline generation is structurally harder.

The gap table below the chart spells out exact coverage and "days until next ramped rep" for each GA date.

Edit Phases / Edit Reps

Edit Phases is grouped by module. Change any Start/End month, name, or color — the Swimlane bar updates immediately. Phases with a teal ⚑ GA milestone badge are the ones that drive GA dates everywhere else (see GA milestone linkage below). Use ↑/↓ to reorder within a module, or "+ Add to [Module]" to insert a new phase.

Edit Reps lets you change name, territory, hire month, Avg ACV, Deals/mo, SS Quota, and status (Active / In training / Planned). Avg ACV and SS Quota display as formatted dollars — click into the field to edit as a plain number, then click away or hit Enter to reformat.

District Buying-Season Multipliers

Texas K-12 districts have a predictable annual rhythm that affects how easy it is to sell, independent of a rep's individual ramp progress:

BUY — Sep, Oct, Jan
Strong buying windows. Fall semester is underway and budgets/grant funds are available; January opens next-year budget planning. Multipliers >1.0.
SLOW — May, Aug, Nov, Dec
Year-end ARDs and graduation (May), back-to-school chaos (Aug), and holiday breaks (Nov-Dec) all reduce admin availability. Multipliers <1.0.
STAAR — Feb, Mar, Apr
State testing prep and administration consumes special ed and assessment staff — your key buyers. Lowest multipliers of the year.
BUDGET — Jun, Jul
Districts finalize budgets for the new fiscal year (Sept 1 start). Mixed access — some bandwidth, some still locked into prior commitments.

These multipliers are editable in the Monthly ARR Build tab ("Edit season multipliers") and apply to every rep's monthly ARR figure when the global toggle is on — including fully-ramped reps. Reset to defaults or zero them out entirely from that same panel.

GA Milestone Linkage

Four phases in Edit Phases are tagged as GA milestones: 504/RTI GA, IEP Module GA, Ed-Fi GA, and LPAC Module GA (marked with a ⚑ badge). Their End month is the single source of truth for that GA date everywhere in this tool.

If the dev team slips a GA date, change that phase's End month in Edit Phases — the swimlane marker, the Readiness Alignment vertical line and coverage colors, the stats bar rep-count card, and the gap table all move together automatically. No other tab needs manual updates.

Scenario Save & Compare

The Scenarios tab lets you capture a full snapshot of the current state — every phase date, every rep's hire date and quota, the global controls, and the season multipliers — under a name you choose.

Save current as scenario — captures everything as it stands right now. Make changes anywhere in the tool (e.g. slip the Dallas Metro hire to January), save again under a different name, and you now have two scenarios.

Load — restores the tool to exactly that scenario's state, across all tabs. Update — overwrites a saved scenario with whatever the working state currently is. Delete — removes it.

Check 2 or more scenarios to see a side-by-side comparison table: FY27 ARR total, delta vs. the base model, training/ramp settings, GA coverage (reps ramped at each milestone), and each rep's hire date and FY27 contribution. The best/worst FY27 totals are highlighted in green/red.

Export / Import — saves all scenarios to a .json file you can keep or share, and load them back in later (e.g. in a future session, since scenarios aren't saved automatically when you close the file).
