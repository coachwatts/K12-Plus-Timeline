Global Controls (top bar)

The five controls in the top-right corner apply across every tab — they're the shared inputs that drive all the calculations.

Start / Months — sets the date range shown on the Swimlane tab only.

Training days / Ramp days — how long a newly hired rep takes to become fully productive. Training is the onboarding period; Ramp is the period after training where productivity scales up gradually (see the ramp curve in Monthly ARR Build). These two numbers add together — e.g. 30 + 90 = 120 days to full productivity.

FY end — the fiscal year-end month used for the Quota and Monthly ARR Build calculations. Defaults to December 2027.

Apply buying-season multiplier — toggles whether the Texas district buying calendar (see below) adjusts the monthly ARR figures. On by default.

★ How the Model Works (read this first)

The whole tool runs on one core idea: a rep's revenue is driven by the pipeline they build, not by a fixed ramp curve. This is the pipeline-balance engine, and it's why hire timing matters so much.

Each month, a rep generates new pipeline (at a rate that ramps up over their first few months), that pipeline accumulates into a running balance, and a fixed percentage of that balance converts to closed ARR each month (the "Pipeline conv. %/mo" control, default 40%). A rep hired in July enters January 2027 with a much larger pipeline balance than one hired in October — so they convert more ARR in FY27 even though both follow the same ramp curve. That gap (often $100K+ per rep) is the single most important thing the model captures.

Two revenue streams feed FY27: new business (reps closing new districts, ~$5.69M at current plan) and expansion (2026 alpha customers upgrading to IEP/LPAC in 2027, ~$77K). The stats bar shows both. Most tabs focus on new business; the Expansion ARR section and ARR Bridge tab bring in the expansion layer.

2026 vs FY27 are different questions. 2026 ARR (the green stat) is constrained by the RTI/504 GA date — only a few invoiceable months this year. FY27 ARR (the amber/pink stats) is the full-year run starting January 2027. The 2026 Forecast tab answers the first; Quota, Monthly Build, Pipeline Required, and ARR Bridge answer the second.

Everything is connected. Change a hire date once (in Quota or Edit Reps) and it ripples through every tab: pipeline balance, FY27 contribution, GA coverage, the bridge, and the what-if baseline all move together.

Swimlane

The master timeline. Top section shows Product Development — grouped by module (Foundation, IEP, Integrations, LPAC) with each phase as a colored bar. Bottom section shows Sales Reps — each rep's Training → Ramping → Full Productivity progression based on their hire date and the global training/ramp settings.

The District Buying Season row near the top shows which months are strong for selling (BUY), slow (SLOW), consumed by STAAR testing (STAAR), or budget-cycle months (BUDGET) — see the dedicated section below.

Dashed teal lines mark GA milestones, derived live from the corresponding phase end-dates in Edit Phases. Hover any bar or band for details.

2026 Forecast

Answers: "What will our actual ARR be for 2026?" — distinct from the FY27 quota model. The core constraint is that RTI/504 isn't invoiceable until the GA date (linked live to the "504 / RTI GA" phase in Edit Phases, currently August 2026), leaving only a handful of billable months in 2026.

Three scenario cards — Conservative, Moderate, Aggressive — apply different conversion rates to the current 25-deal pipeline (Signed/Active/Proposed) and add a configurable number of new districts sourced from TEPSA and the upcoming TCASE conference. Each card shows the math breakdown and a "full run-rate if sustained" reference figure.

The Scenario Assumptions grid lets you edit conversion percentages, new-district counts, billable months for new districts, and the shared average new-district ARR — everything recalculates live. The pipeline table below shows all 25 deals with editable ARR values; the 5 Demo-stage deals show "TBD" until quantified.

The stats bar's "2026 ARR (Moderate)" card and the Scenarios tab's comparison both reflect this tab's Moderate scenario for quick reference.

Expansion ARR (bottom of 2026 Forecast)

Answers: "How much extra ARR comes from upselling our existing customers?" When IEP and LPAC go GA in January 2027, every 2026 alpha district that's live on RTI/504 becomes an upgrade target. This is the land-and-expand half of the growth story.

Three scenario cards model how many of the 17 alpha logos upgrade and how big the lift is: Conservative ($27K, 30% adopt IEP only), Moderate ($77K, 45% adopt IEP + partial LPAC — this matches the deck), and Aggressive ($150K, 65% adopt full bundle). Each card shows the resulting net revenue retention (NRR) for that cohort.

The adopt rate % and lift per district $ are editable per scenario. The alpha customer table lists all 17 logos with current ARR and max expansion potential. Wall ISD is the live proof point: it added RTI to an existing 504 contract and hit 158% NRR in Year 1 — before IEP/LPAC even existed.

The Moderate expansion figure flows into the stats bar's "FY27 — incl. expansion" card, the ARR Bridge, and the What-If tab's expansion line.

Pipeline Required

Answers: "How much pipeline does each rep need to hit their number, and how far are we from it?" This is the demand-side mirror of the quota model (originally Slide 21 of the deck).

For each rep: Pipeline needed = Deals/mo × Sales cycle months × Avg ACV. Two editable cycle controls at the top set the assumption — Mid/Ent reps default to a 9-month cycle, Velocity reps to 4 months (smaller districts, faster decisions). The table shows opps needed, pipeline ARR needed, the balance already built by January 2027 (from the pipeline-balance model + hire dates), and the resulting gap.

The proof-point callout cites real results: one AE built $163K of ARR pipeline in 17.7 weeks via cold sequences alone, before any tradeshow leads — evidence that the pipeline generation assumption is grounded, not optimistic. The per-rep progress bars show each rep's balance building toward their threshold month by month.

Change a hire date and the "Balance Jan '27" and "Gap" columns update live — earlier hires close more of the gap before FY27 starts.

Quota & ARR Impact

Shows each rep's Avg ACV, Deals/mo, and Steady-State Quota (their full annual quota once fully ramped), alongside Base FY27 (the original mid-year model figure) and Current FY27 (live-calculated from the rep's hire date through the pipeline-balance model — the pipeline they build, ramp, and convert, adjusted by the buying-season multiplier if enabled).

Because FY27 now runs on the pipeline-balance engine, the Current FY27 figure is systematically more conservative than the old straight-line ramp model, and it's far more sensitive to hire timing. You can edit a rep's hire date directly in this table — the totals, the delta vs. base model, and every other tab update immediately. This is the fastest way to test "what if this hire slips a month?" and watch the FY27 number move.

For 2026 revenue (as opposed to FY27 quota), see the 2026 Forecast tab. For the pipeline each rep needs to hit these numbers, see Pipeline Required.

Monthly ARR Build

The most detailed view. The chart shows monthly (or cumulative) ARR contribution stacked by rep, against the $7.1M annual / $591K monthly target line. The "Monthly view start" control (top bar) lets you show pre-FY27 months so a July or August hire's pipeline build-up is visible; months before January 2027 are dimmed and excluded from the FY27 total.

Below the chart, four tables: the ramp curve (productivity % by month-since-hire, a reference for how training/ramp days stretch the curve), the pipeline balance per rep per month (the orange table — this is the engine: watch each rep's accumulated pipeline build from their hire date), ramp % per rep per month, and monthly ARR per rep from the pipeline-balance model with a team total row colored against the target.

The pipeline balance table is the clearest place to see why hire timing matters: a July hire's balance line starts three months before an October hire's, so it's already converting at a higher rate when FY27 begins. Use Edit season multipliers to open the inline editor for the 12 monthly buying-season multipliers.

Readiness Alignment

Answers: "Will we have enough ramped reps when a product hits GA?" Each GA milestone is a vertical line, colored green (2+ reps ramped), amber (1 rep), or red (0 reps) based on coverage at that date. Rep rows below show their hire → train → ramp → full progression on the same timeline.

The background shading and bottom strip show the buying-season overlay — useful for spotting cases where a rep's ramp period lands during STAAR testing season, when pipeline generation is structurally harder.

The gap table below the chart spells out exact coverage and "days until next ramped rep" for each GA date.

ARR Bridge

Answers: "How do we get from where we are in 2026 to the FY27 target?" This is the investor-facing storytelling view (Slides 10–13 of the deck).

The Two Unlocks panel frames the growth: AE Scale (1.5 FTE → 6-8 ramped reps, ACV rising from $6-8K to $22-80K as territory mix improves) and Module Expansion (IEP+LPAC launch lifting avg ACV from $6.9K to $20.5K). The visual bridge walks left to right: 2026 EOY run-rate → AE scale → new business ARR → + expansion → − attrition → FY27 plan, with horizontal lines marking the Floor ($5.3M), Target ($7.1M), and Upside ($9.5M) scenarios.

The Floor / Target / Upside comparison shows your live current-plan number against all three deck scenarios, with a full reconciliation ladder: new business + expansion − churn − rep attrition = net FY27 ARR. The churn % and rep attrition % are editable inline — set them above zero to stress-test the plan. Logos count, total booked revenue, and comp/ARR ratio are shown per scenario.

Right now the current plan sits above Floor but below Target. Open the What-If tab and raise the win-rate or deal-size lever to see the bridge close the gap to Target.

What-If

Answers: "What's the combined revenue impact if several things change at once?" Seven sliders, all applied simultaneously, with a live baseline-vs-what-if comparison.

The levers: GA acceleration (more 2026 invoiceable months — FY26 only), hire acceleration (earlier hires build more pipeline before FY27), win rate and deal size (both scale rep quota and flow through the pipeline model), pipeline volume (faster balance build-up), sales cycle speed (faster conversion, also shrinks the pipeline gap), and module adoption (scales the IEP/LPAC expansion ARR).

The results table shows FY26 (all three scenarios), FY27 new business, expansion ARR, FY27 incl. expansion, and the pipeline gap — each with baseline, what-if, and dollar/percent deltas. The baseline FY27-incl-expansion figure reconciles exactly with the ARR Bridge. The per-lever waterfall isolates each lever's individual contribution so you can see which moves the needle most.

For reference: win rate and deal size are the biggest single FY27 levers (~$1.1M each at 1.2×); sales cycle speed has the biggest effect on the pipeline gap; module adoption is the only lever that moves the expansion line.

Edit Phases / Edit Reps

Edit Phases is grouped by module. Change any Start/End month, name, or color — the Swimlane bar updates immediately. Phases with a teal ⚑ GA milestone badge are the ones that drive GA dates everywhere else (see GA milestone linkage below). Use ↑/↓ to reorder within a module, or "+ Add to [Module]" to insert a new phase.

Edit Reps lets you change name, territory, hire month, Avg ACV, Deals/mo, SS Quota, and status (Active / In training / Planned). Avg ACV and SS Quota display as formatted dollars — click into the field to edit as a plain number, then click away or hit Enter to reformat.

District Buying-Season Multipliers

Texas K-12 districts have a predictable annual rhythm that affects how easy it is to sell, independent of a rep's individual ramp progress:

BUY — Sep, Oct, Jan
Strong buying windows. Fall semester is underway and budgets/grant funds are available; January opens next-year budget planning. Multipliers >1.0.
SLOW — May, Aug, Nov, Dec
Year-end ARDs and graduation (May), back-to-school chaos (Aug), and holiday breaks (Nov-Dec) all reduce admin availability. Multipliers <1.0.
STAAR — Feb, Mar, Apr
State testing prep and administration consumes special ed and assessment staff — your key buyers. Lowest multipliers of the year.
BUDGET — Jun, Jul
Districts finalize budgets for the new fiscal year (Sept 1 start). Mixed access — some bandwidth, some still locked into prior commitments.

These multipliers are editable in the Monthly ARR Build tab ("Edit season multipliers") and apply to every rep's monthly ARR figure when the global toggle is on — including fully-ramped reps. Reset to defaults or zero them out entirely from that same panel.

GA Milestone Linkage

Four phases in Edit Phases are tagged as GA milestones: 504/RTI GA, IEP Module GA, Ed-Fi GA, and LPAC Module GA (marked with a ⚑ badge). Their End month is the single source of truth for that GA date everywhere in this tool.

If the dev team slips a GA date, change that phase's End month in Edit Phases — the swimlane marker, the Readiness Alignment vertical line and coverage colors, the stats bar rep-count card, and the gap table all move together automatically. No other tab needs manual updates.

Scenario Save & Compare

The Scenarios tab lets you capture a full snapshot of the current state under a name you choose. A snapshot now captures everything: every phase date, every rep's hire date and quota, the global controls (including pipeline conversion rate), the season multipliers, the 2026 forecast assumptions, the expansion assumptions, the sales-cycle settings, the What-If levers, and the bridge churn/attrition inputs.

Save current as scenario — captures everything as it stands right now. Make changes anywhere (e.g. slip the Dallas Metro hire to January, or bump expansion to aggressive), save again under a different name, and you have two fully independent scenarios.

Load — restores the tool to exactly that scenario's state across all eleven tabs. Update — overwrites a saved scenario with the current working state. Delete — removes it.

Check 2 or more scenarios to see a side-by-side comparison: 2026 ARR, FY27 new business, expansion ARR, FY27 incl. expansion, delta vs. base model, training/ramp settings, GA coverage, and each rep's hire date and FY27 contribution. Best/worst FY27 totals are highlighted green/red.

Export / Import — saves all scenarios to a .json file you can keep or share, and load them back later (scenarios aren't saved automatically when you close the file).
